## Moscow: History and Modern Life

Moscow is the capital of Russia. Many people call it the heart of the country. Prince Yury Dolgoruky started the city in 1147. 

In the past, people built houses from wood. Later, they started using white stone. In 1812, there was a big fire during the war with Napoleon. It destroyed many buildings. After the fire, people rebuilt the city with new stone houses.

Today, Moscow is a very big city. More than 10 million people live there. The city has many modern factories, offices, and sports centers.

Traffic is a big problem because the city is so large. The Metro is the best way to travel. The government is building new roads to make travel easier.

Moscow is a modern city with great hotels and shops. But it still keeps its history. It is a friendly and welcoming place for everyone.

## Computers and the Internet

Computers are very important today. We use them everywhere: in schools, hospitals, and offices. They help us work fast and well. We use them to study and have fun.

The Internet connects computers all over the world. It is very useful because we can find a lot of information and talk to people fast.

But computers also have some problems:
Firstly, Health: If you sit at a computer for a long time, your eyes can hurt. You may feel very tired.
Secondly, Addiction: Many people, especially kids, cannot live without games or the internet. 
Thirdly, Crime: Bad people can use computers to steal your data or money.

To sum up, the computer is a great thing, but we must use it carefully.
