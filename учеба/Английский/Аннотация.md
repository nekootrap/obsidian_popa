# 3 Unit

The text is titled "The First Computers".

The text is devoted to the history of old computers. The text is about the first machines and the people who made them.

At the beginning of the text the author mentions Vannevar Bush. He tells us that Bush made the first analog computer in 1930 to help during the war.

The aim of the text is to provide the reader some information on new technology in the 1940s and 1950s. It describes the Mark I, the first digital computer. It also explains the ideas of John von Neumann about computer memory.

At the end of the text the author mentions vacuum tubes. He draws the conclusion that these tubes made computers very fast at that time.

I found the text interesting and easy to understand.

In my opinion, this information is very useful for students. I believe that it is good to know how computers started.
